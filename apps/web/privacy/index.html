Privacy

Your ranking stays with you.

AllTime25 is a client-only website. Your selections and completed ranking are not uploaded to an AllTime25 server.

Browser storage

Your current ranking is saved in localStorage so you can close the page and resume later on the same browser.

No accounts

AllTime25 does not use accounts, ranking cookies, or a database of user rankings.

No device sync

Rankings do not sync between browsers or devices because they are not uploaded.

Deleting progress

Restarting replaces your ranking. Clearing this site’s browser data deletes it completely.
